# BizSpeak Practice Room – MVP Project Summary

## Vision

BizSpeak Practice Room is a daily English speaking habit platform for Chinese professionals (30–40 years old) who have strong reading/writing skills but weak speaking confidence.

The goal is not to teach English grammar.

The goal is to build a consistent speaking habit through:

* Curated videos
* Daily spoken summaries
* Accountability
* Community discussion
* Visible progress

We are validating whether busy professionals will consistently:

Watch → Speak → Submit → Return Tomorrow

before investing heavily in AI.

---

# Target Audience

Chinese professionals aged 30–40:

* Managers
* E-commerce operators
* Manufacturers
* Export/import professionals
* Parents with international exposure

Common characteristics:

* Strong reading/writing
* Weak speaking/listening
* Fear of mistakes
* Translation mindset
* Limited time
* Want career ROI

## Recording System

Users can:

* Delete recording

---

# Deliberate MVP Decision

AI feedback has been removed.

Reason:

The biggest risk is not AI quality.

The biggest risk is whether users return consistently.

Current focus:

Habit formation.

The weekly discussion session provides feedback manually.

---

# Product Philosophy

People do not buy AI.

People buy:

* Confidence
* Accountability
* Progress
* Community
* Consistency

The discussion session may become more valuable than AI.

---

# Current Validation Goal

We are not validating AI.

We are validating:

Can 7 professionals complete a 5-day speaking challenge and attend discussion sessions?

Success metric:

* Daily recording completion
* Repeat usage
* Discussion attendance
* User engagement

# MVP Success Definition

A successful MVP is NOT:

"AI gives amazing feedback."

A successful MVP is:

"Users consistently return, practice speaking, and engage in discussions."

If users build a habit, AI can be added later.

If users do not build a habit, AI will not solve the problem.
